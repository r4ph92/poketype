# Local game artwork

- `scenes/`: original vector environment, separate from UI text.
- `sprites/trainers/red.png` and `oak.png`: official trainer artwork obtained from Pokémon Showdown. Render at the native 80×80 size or an integer multiple. Source and rights notes are in `src/assets/manifest.ts`.
- `sprites/pokemon/<species>/`: unmodified PMDCollab Walk and Idle sheets, original `AnimData.xml`, and original `credits.txt` for every species of Pokédex #1–649 that has them upstream (635 of 649). Some source actions explicitly alias another action; the importer and generator resolve those references without duplicating or modifying sheets. The 14 species without Idle and Walk sheets upstream (Tranquill, Blitzle, Zebstrika, Throh, Simisear, Simipour, Crustle, Tirtouga, Carracosta, Amoonguss, Frillish, Shelmet, Stunfisk, Bouffalant) are shown with their still front artwork and marked `animation: 'still'` in `src/assets/pokemon-credits.ts` and on `/credits`; a folder may still hold their partial upstream XML and credits.

`src/assets/pokemon.ts` (frame sizes and timings for the browser), `src/assets/pokemon-credits.ts` (source, authors and credits file per species, read on the server) and `src/assets/pokedex.ts` are generated from those local files by `python3 scripts/build-sprite-metadata.py`. They retain actual frame dimensions and per-frame duration (1/60 second); `PokemonSprite` builds one keyframe animation per timing at render time. Rows are south, southwest, west, northwest, north, northeast, east, southeast. No sprite is stretched or recolored.

Most imported Walk and Idle animations are credited to CHUNSOFT with `Unspecified` terms upstream; action-specific community contributors are preserved in the source credits and named in the generated manifest. Community contributions in SpriteCollab have their own terms, but that does not relicense these official sheets. Pokémon remains third-party IP; the project-wide non-commercial educational notice applies. The in-app `/credits` page includes an expandable 649-species attribution list grouped by region, naming authors and linking each source.

The old `trainer.svg` and static `charmander.png` are retained as unused prior-review assets. They no longer render in gameplay.

Each player's saved partner is the species shown for them on the roster, route, leader bar, results and podium; a race keeps the partner recorded at its start. A member without a starter shows no Pokémon.

During a race, idle sheets are frozen. Only an increase in correct-character progress triggers the short walk cycle and route interpolation. Wrong characters, ignored inputs, unchanged poll responses, and waiting do not trigger motion. Camp, lobby, and podium sprites stay still. Race walk cadence scales with recent correct-character input (including batched remote progress), with a bounded playback rate; only races show motion controls. The credits gallery explicitly previews the available animations.

The trainer dashboard adds the original `scenes/camp.svg` and eight original vector badge icons under `badges/` (Volcano and Earth drawn for PokéType in the same 32×32 flat pixel style as the first six). Unmodified 96×96 front sprites under `sprites/pokemon/front/` come from PokeAPI's Generation V Black/White sprite archive; their source template, IDs, and rights notes are recorded in `dashboardArtwork` in `src/assets/manifest.ts`.

Trainer statistics, recent races, badges and the collection come from the server (`src/server/trainer/`). Choosing a partner in PC storage is saved on the server. Trainer (`/play`), Collection (`/play/collection`), Race (`/play/race`) and Routes (`/play/routes`) are separate pages, using the shared `SiteHeader`/`SiteNav` and `SiteFooter` components. The real race creation action remains connected. The practice dialog is local and grants no XP or rewards. `src/assets/racer-portraits.ts` (podium crops of all 649 front sprites) is generated by `npx tsx scripts/build-racer-portraits.ts`.

## Shop items

`items/` holds 26 original 32×32 pixel icons drawn for PokéType in the flat style of the badges, one per shop item: the nine evolution stones (Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf, Moon, and since 2026-10-02 Sun, Shiny, Dusk, Dawn), the fifteen evolution items (Metal Coat, King's Rock, Dragon Scale, Up-Grade, Dubious Disc, Protector, Electirizer, Magmarizer, Reaper Cloth, Razor Claw, Razor Fang, Oval Stone, Deep Sea Tooth, Deep Sea Scale, Prism Scale), the Poké Ball and the PC Box. They are listed in `itemArtwork` in `src/assets/manifest.ts` (names from the shop catalogue) and credited on `/credits`.

## Saved trainer appearance

`sprites/trainers/overworld/` contains six unmodified 144×32 sheets from `pret/pokeemerald` (official Nintendo / Game Freak / The Pokémon Company artwork). `src/assets/trainers.ts` records the exact upstream paths. Each frame is 16×32; `OverworldTrainer` renders integer-scaled frames and applies the source game's palette-zero transparency rule at runtime. Camp uses a side-facing frame, while the chooser shows front-facing frames.

Signup now redirects to `/choose-trainer`. The allowlisted selection is saved to `users.trainer_avatar` through the current authenticated session and displayed beside the partner at camp. Migration `0008_overjoyed_catseye.sql` adds the nullable appearance column, so older accounts keep the default Red until they choose a trainer. Trainer appearance, collection and partner are persisted; XP is not granted yet. The dashboard appearance link lets existing accounts choose or change their sprite.

`/play/choose-starter` offers Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle in shuffled order to every new player (guest or account) and saves the choice on the server as their first Pokémon and partner.

## Lobby roster

The waiting-room UI displays live membership in a responsive roster (eight desktop columns, two mobile columns), verified with 32 test participants. Each card opens a keyboard-accessible profile dialog. Saved account trainer appearances are included in room snapshots; guests use Red. Each card shows the member's saved partner; the profile dialog shows the partner's level and the member's real races, wins, average WPM and accuracy (a dash until a race is ranked).

The invitation panel displays the full existing room UUID and the working copy-invite-link control. No short-code lookup service or new capacity limit was introduced. The 32-player test checks roster rendering and starting a race, not sustained multiplayer load capacity.


## 649-species catalog and achievement preview

- `/play/pokedex`: paginated searchable catalog of Pokédex #1–649, one region at a time (Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova) with each region's caught count, ownership/evolution/legendary filters, source-backed evolution predecessors, and opt-in race animation previews. All species have native front artwork plus directional idle and walk metadata, including evolved forms and five legendary/mythical species.
- `/play/achievements`: the eight badge milestones (`gameConfig.badges`, granted at race settlement) and five legendary goals in `src/config/achievements.ts`, with the trainer's real progress. Legendary rewards are not granted yet.
- `scripts/data/<region>.json` (kanto, johto, hoenn, sinnoh, unova): official localized names, evolution predecessors and wild tiers, generated by `python3 scripts/import-species-catalog.py` from PokeAPI CSVs and `scripts/data/species-rules.json`.
- `python3 scripts/import-pokemon-sprites.py` imports unmodified sheets and front artwork for all 649 species and refreshes `scripts/data/sprite-credit-names.txt`. `python3 scripts/build-sprite-metadata.py` validates dimensions, frame timing and attribution, handles `CopyOf`, and rebuilds TypeScript/CSS/catalog names.
- `scripts/data/sprite-credit-names.txt` resolves upstream contributor IDs. Original per-species credits and `SOURCE-LICENSE.md` remain available locally. Community asset terms do not relicense official game artwork.
