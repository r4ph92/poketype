# Pokémon progression rules

Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-30. All values live in one typed configuration file so they can be tuned without code changes. Species and evolution never change race speed: position comes only from typing.

## Race XP by result

Decided on 2026-10-02; replaces the place table of 2026-09-30. XP goes to the Pokémon recorded as racer when the race started (`race_participants.partner_dex`), even if the partner changes during the race. The server grants it inside the race settlement, once per race participant (unique row in the `xp_grants` ledger).

`XP = 50 + 100 × racers beaten ÷ (racers − 1)`, rounded.

- Racers are everyone who started the race, abandoners included. Racers beaten = racers − place, so the winner earns 150 and the last 50 whatever the field size.
- Tied racers share the better place (1, 1, 3) and its XP.
- Racers the time limit stopped are ranked by score (H3) and use the same formula.
- An abandoner earns 0 and counts as beaten by everyone still racing.
- A race counts only with at least 2 racers.

| Racers | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th | 8th |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 150 | 50 | | | | | | |
| 3 | 150 | 100 | 50 | | | | | |
| 4 | 150 | 117 | 83 | 50 | | | | |
| 8 | 150 | 136 | 121 | 107 | 93 | 79 | 64 | 50 |

Values: `gameConfig.progression.raceXp` in `src/config/game.ts`.

## Levels

Every Pokémon starts at level 1 with 0 XP and caps at level 99. XP to reach the next level is `50 + 10 × current level` (60 at level 1, 1 030 at level 98; 53 410 XP in total from level 1 to 99). One race can raise several levels; XP beyond level 99 is not stored. The migration that introduced these rules reset every owned Pokémon to level 1 with 0 XP; the `ash` seed puts its Pokémon at the level cap (99).

## Evolution

A Pokémon evolves automatically, inside the same settlement, when its level reaches the evolution level of the original games. The owned Pokémon becomes the evolved species and keeps its level and XP; several steps happen in one grant when the level allows. The Pokédex keeps the old form caught.

- Data: `src/assets/level-evolutions.ts`, generated by `python3 scripts/import-level-evolutions.py` from the PokéAPI evolution table (Generations I–V rules; the shared rules live in `scripts/evolution_source.py`). Each entry has a trigger: `level` keeps the original game level; `friendship`, `move` and `location` happen at the levels in `gameConfig.evolution`.
- Since 2026-10-02 (Johto to Unova, #1–649):
  - Babies never evolve by level: Pichu, Mélo/Cleffa, Toudoudou/Igglybuff, Togepi, Debugant/Tyrogue, Lippouti/Smoochum, Élekid, Magby, Azurill, Okéoké/Wynaut, Rozbouton/Budew, Korillon/Chingling, Manzaï/Bonsly, Mime Jr., Ptiravi/Happiny, Goinfrex/Munchlax, Babimanta/Mantyke. Their evolved forms (Pikachu, Mélofée, Rondoudou, Togetic, Kicklee, Tygnon, Kapoera, Lippoutou, Élektek, Magmar, Marill, Qulbutoké, Rosélia, Éoko, Simularbre, M. Mime, Leveinard, Ronflex, Démanta) spawn in the wild as base forms and keep their own later evolutions. The babies spawn too, so the Pokédex can be completed. Exception: Ptiravi evolves into Leveinard with the Pierre Ovale item (below).
  - Friendship evolutions happen at level 30 (`friendshipLevel`): Nostenfer/Crobat, Leuphorie/Blissey, Lockpin/Lopunny, Lucario, Rhinolove/Swoobat, Manternel/Leavanny, and Eevee's Espeon/Umbreon.
  - Move evolutions happen at level 33 (`moveLevel`): Coudlangue → Excelangue, Saquedeneu → Bouldeneu, Yanma → Yanmega, Cochignon → Mammochon, Capumain → Capidextre.
  - Eevee: the Fire, Water and Thunder stones still choose Flareon, Vaporeon and Jolteon. An Eevee that reaches level 30 without a stone evolves at random into Mentali/Espeon, Noctali/Umbreon, Phyllali/Leafeon or Givrali/Glaceon (`locationLevel` 30 for the last two), drawn by the server's random source.
  - Several evolutions reached at the same level are drawn at random by the server: Chenipotte/Wurmple → Armulys/Silcoon or Blindalys/Cascoon at 7, Cheniti/Burmy → Cheniselle/Wormadam or Papilord/Mothim at 20 (no gender in PokéType). Apitrini/Combee always becomes Apireine/Vespiquen at 21.
  - Location evolutions use the Thunder Stone, as in recent games: Magnéton → Magnézone, Tarinor → Tarinorme.
  - Pure trade evolutions wait for trading: Kadabra, Machopeur, Gravalanch, Spectrum, Géolithe/Boldore, Ouvrifier/Gurdurr, Carabing/Karrablast and Escargaume/Shelmet. Munja/Shedinja (Ningale's extra Pokémon) is not obtainable yet.
- Stone and item evolutions happen only when the trainer uses one (see "Evolution stones and items" below).
- The cap was raised from 50 to 99 on 2026-10-02 so Dragonair can evolve into Dragonite at its game level, 55.
- A collection may hold several Pokémon of one species (since 2026-10-02): a Pokémon evolves even when the trainer already owns the evolved form.

The results screen replays the grant from the ledger: "+X XP", the bar filling from the old to the new XP, a flash of each level reached and the evolution sequence. A reload shows the final state without replaying it or granting again; with reduced motion the final state shows at once.

## Poké Dollars (₽)

Decided on 2026-10-02. Each trainer (guest identity, so the wallet follows the guest into an account) holds a Poké Dollar balance (`guests.coins`, never below zero).

- After each race: `₽ = 100 + 100 × racers beaten ÷ (racers − 1)`, rounded, the same shape and field rules as the XP: racers are everyone who started, tied racers share the better place, an abandoner gets 0 ₽, and a race pays only with at least 2 racers. The winner earns 200 ₽ and the last 100 ₽.
- Each badge earned pays a 500 ₽ bonus, once, at the settlement that grants it. Badges earned before this rule (or seeded) pay nothing retroactively.
- Every change goes through the `coin_entries` ledger: a race payout is unique per race participant and a badge bonus unique per trainer and badge, so a repeated or concurrent settlement pays nothing more. The results screen shows "+200 ₽" (and "Bonus de badge : +500 ₽") under the XP, read from the ledger; a reload shows the same and pays nothing. The hub shows the balance.

A purchase is one item and a quantity from 1 to 99, confirmed in a dialog. Bought items go to the bag (`bag_items`, shown in the shop), except a PC Box, which adds a box to the trainer's PC (see "PC boxes"). Poké Balls are thrown at wild encounters.

## Evolution stones and items

A stone or an evolution item is used from the collection: the Pokémon's detail lists the ones it reacts to, with "Utiliser : Pierre Feu" when the bag holds one, or a link to the shop when it does not. The Pokémon becomes the evolved species, keeps its level, XP and partner place, and the item is consumed; the evolution scene of the results screen plays (final form at once under reduced motion). The Pokédex keeps the old form caught (table `stone_evolutions`, kept under its first name; `itemEvolutions` in the schema).

- Data: `src/assets/item-evolutions.ts`, generated by `python3 scripts/import-item-evolutions.py` from the PokéAPI evolution and item tables (Generations I–V). An item the games hold during a trade or a level-up is used like a stone: no trade needed.
- Kanto stones: Fire (Vulpix, Growlithe, Eevee → Flareon, Pansear), Water (Poliwhirl, Shellder, Staryu, Eevee → Vaporeon, Lombre, Panpour), Thunder (Pikachu, Eevee → Jolteon, Eelektrik, Magneton, Nosepass), Leaf (Gloom, Weepinbell, Exeggcute, Nuzleaf, Pansage), Moon (Nidorina, Nidorino, Clefairy, Jigglypuff, Skitty, Munna).
- New stones: Sun (Gloom → Bellossom, Sunkern, Cottonee, Petilil), Shiny (Togetic, Roselia, Minccino), Dusk (Murkrow, Misdreavus, Lampent), Dawn (Kirlia → Gallade, Snorunt → Froslass; no gender in PokéType).
- Evolution items: Peau Métal / Metal Coat (Onix, Scyther), Roche Royale / King's Rock (Poliwhirl → Politoed, Slowpoke → Slowking), Écaille Draco / Dragon Scale (Seadra), Améliorator / Up-Grade (Porygon), CD Douteux / Dubious Disc (Porygon2), Protecteur / Protector (Rhydon), Électriseur / Electirizer (Electabuzz), Magmariseur / Magmarizer (Magmar), Tissu Fauche / Reaper Cloth (Dusclops), Griffe Rasoir / Razor Claw (Sneasel), Croc Rasoir / Razor Fang (Gligar), Pierre Ovale / Oval Stone (Happiny), Dent Océan / Deep Sea Tooth and Écaille Océan / Deep Sea Scale (Clamperl → Huntail or Gorebyss), Bel'Écaille / Prism Scale (Feebas).
- Eevee lists its three stones and the trainer chooses one; Poliwhirl, Gloom, Clamperl and Slowpoke also have two ways.
- The item works even when the trainer already owns the evolved form; the collection then holds both.

## Wild encounters

Decided on 2026-10-02; replaces the 25% post-race drop proposal. Values: `gameConfig.encounters` in `src/config/game.ts`.

- Who: in a race of at least 2 racers, the first three places always meet one wild Pokémon (tied racers share the better place); every other ranked racer meets one with a 10% chance; an abandoner never does. The server draws it inside the race settlement, once per race participant (unique row in `wild_encounters`), with server randomness (`src/server/random.ts`, injectable for tests).
- What: base forms only from every region, Kanto to Unova (evolved forms are only obtained by evolving; a baby's evolved form counts as a base form), starters included; the five Kanto legendaries never appear in the wild. The species is drawn by rarity tier, then uniformly within the tier; the level is random from 1 to 5. Tiers live in species data (`rarity` in `src/assets/pokedex.ts`, from `scripts/data/species-rules.json`: every wild species not listed is common).

| Tier | Weight | Catch chance per throw | Species |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 60 | 70% | 84 base forms such as Rattata, Pidgey, Sentret, Zigzagoon, Bidoof, Patrat |
| Uncommon | 30 | 45% | 149 base forms such as Pikachu, Growlithe, Mareep, Ralts, Shinx, Drilbur |
| Rare | 10 | 20% | 67 base forms: the 21 starters, fossils, pseudo-legendary bases (Dratini, Larvitar, Bagon, Beldum, Gible, Deino), the 17 babies, Eevee, Porygon, Lapras, Snorlax, Feebas, Riolu, Rotom, Zorua, Larvesta… |
| Legendary | 1 | 5%, one throw only | the 43 legendaries and mythicals of Johto to Unova (Raikou, Entei, Suicune, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Celebi, Regis, Latias, Latios, Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza, Jirachi, Deoxys, the lake trio, Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Heatran, Regigigas, Cresselia, Phione, Manaphy, Darkrai, Shaymin, Arceus, Victini, the Swords of Justice, the forces of nature, Reshiram, Zekrom, Kyurem, Keldeo, Meloetta, Genesect) |

Tiers follow availability in the original games. A legendary rolls about 1 encounter in 101 and flees as soon as its single Poké Ball misses (`legendaryThrows`).

- Queue: encounters wait on the trainer hub until used, oldest first, at most 10; an encounter drawn while 10 wait is dropped. The results screen shows "Un Pokémon sauvage apparaît !" with the species, its level and a link to the hub encounter.
- Catching: up to 3 throws per encounter, each spending one Poké Ball. The server rolls each throw; a catch adds the Pokémon at the encounter's level with 0 XP and pays no XP and no Poké Dollars. After the third miss the Pokémon flees. "Laisser partir" closes the encounter. Without a Poké Ball, or with a full PC, the throw is refused, no ball is spent and the encounter keeps waiting.
- Each throw form names the throw it means (1 to 3) and the server records it once (`encounter_throws`), under the trainer's row lock, so a double click spends one ball and answers the same outcome twice.

## PC boxes

- A collection can hold several Pokémon of one species. The PC starts with 1 box of 30 places (`gameConfig.storage`); the shop sells more boxes at 200 ₽ each. The collection page shows one box at a time with its free places and "x / y places".
- A full PC refuses catches and keeps earned legendaries waiting (below) until a box is bought or a Pokémon released.
- "Relâcher" releases a Pokémon after a confirmation; the partner can never be released. The Pokédex keeps a released species caught (`released_pokemon`).
- The migration gave every existing trainer enough boxes for the Pokémon they already held.

## Kanto legendaries

Kanto's five legendaries stay badge rewards and never appear in the wild; the legendaries of Johto to Unova are very rare wild encounters (see "Wild encounters"). Granted for real since 2026-10-02. After each race settlement, the server checks each participant against the goals in `src/config/achievements.ts` (tunable; to be revised with the product owner). A legendary whose goals are all met is granted once per trainer (primary key in `legendary_grants`) and joins the collection at level 5; the results screen announces "Artikodin rejoint ton équipe !". When the PC is full, the legendary is earned but waits outside the collection ("… t'attend") and is placed automatically, still once, as soon as a place frees (a box bought or a Pokémon released). The achievements page shows each legendary as obtained, waiting, reached (it arrives after the next race) or locked. A released legendary is never granted again.

## Saved collection — 2026-10-01

- Starter: on first entry every player, guest or account, picks Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle (`gameConfig.progression.starterDexes`). The server saves it once at level 1 with 0 XP (level 5 before 2026-10-02) as the partner and refuses any other species or a second starter. Creating a room, "Faire une course", a room link, a room code and the trainer hub all send a player without a starter to `/play/choose-starter` first, then carry on to where they were going.
- Collection: one row per Pokémon per guest identity (`owned_pokemon`; one row per species until 2026-10-02), so an account keeps the collection its guest built (the account reuses the guest id, inside the account-creation transaction). Logging into an existing account shows that account's own collection; the guest's is not merged (H2). Exactly one partner per trainer, enforced by a partial unique index.
- Partner: chosen among owned Pokémon in PC storage and saved on the server. Each race records each participant's partner at the start (`race_participants.partner_dex`), so the race map, leader bar, results, podium and past results keep the Pokémon that raced. Species never change speed.
- Statistics come from settled races only: races, wins, average WPM and accuracy average the ranked results; the hub lists the last three races with the XP each granted ("—" for a race settled before XP existed).
- Badges: the eight badges (`gameConfig.badges`, adding Volcano and Earth to the first six) are granted once, inside the race settlement, at 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 75, 100 and 150 races finished to the end. A racer stopped by the time limit or who abandoned earns no badge from that race. A missed milestone catches up at the next finished race.
- Legendary goals show the trainer's real progress and grant the legendary (see "Kanto legendaries").
- Test account: `ash` (normal rights) holds all 151 species at level 99 with Pikachu as partner, all eight badges, the five legendaries recorded as granted, 6 PC boxes, 99 999 ₽, 10 of each stone and 50 Poké Balls, created (or topped up) by the seed when `SEED_ASH_PASSWORD` is set.
